Questions

What clients ask before the first call

Draft answers. to be reviewed by advisors

Will I get approved?

Can foreigners get a mortgage in Dubai without UAE residency?

Yes. Several UAE banks lend to non-residents, usually up to 50–60% of the property value, with higher income and balance requirements than for residents.

Can I get a mortgage if I'm self-employed?

Yes. Most banks want around two years of company history and audited accounts or bank statements. Some accept a younger business at a lower loan-to-value.

My bank declined me. Is that the end?

Rarely. Each bank has its own rules on income, nationality, employer and credit history. We find out why you were declined and which bank's rules fit your case.

How much cash do I need?

What do I need besides the down payment?

The 4% DLD transfer fee, trustee and mortgage registration fees, the agent's commission, the valuation and the bank's processing fee. Banks don't finance these, so they come from your savings.

What hidden costs do people miss?

Most often the valuation gap: when the bank values the property below the price, you pay the difference. Also life and property insurance, and early settlement fees if you sell or refinance early.

How long does it take?

Do I need to be in Dubai to apply?

For pre-approval, no. Most of the process runs remotely. Signing at the transfer can usually be done through a power of attorney. confirm

How long is a pre-approval valid?

30 to 60 days, depending on the bank. We time it so it doesn't expire before you find the property.

Why Lenty?

Is a broker really better than going to my bank?

Your bank offers one product. We compare banks for your profile and handle the parts that usually stall a complex file: income structure, documents and valuation timing.

Do you also sell property?

No. We only do mortgages. That's why agents trust us with their clients.

Non-resident mortgages

Buy in Dubai while you live abroad

You don't need UAE residency for a Dubai mortgage. You need the bank whose rules fit your country, your currency and your income. That's the part we handle.

Who this is for

  • You live and earn outside the UAE and want to buy a home or an investment property in Dubai.
  • Your income is in EUR, GBP, USD or another major currency.
  • You own a business abroad, or you're salaried with payslips and bank statements.
  • Your off-plan handover payment is coming up and you need a mortgage for it.

How much cash you'll need

Non-residents usually borrow up to 50–60% of the price. Off-plan properties are capped at 50% until completion. On top of the down payment come the fees banks don't finance.

Example, AED 2,000,000 ready apartment, 50% financing: down payment 1,000,000 + DLD 80,580 + agent 42,000 + registration, valuation and bank fees ≈ 20,500. Total ≈ AED 1.14M.

Documents banks usually ask for

Salaried

  • Passport (all pages) and national ID
  • 6 months of payslips and a salary certificate
  • 6 months of personal bank statements
  • Proof of address
  • Credit report from your country, for some banks

Business owner

  • Passport and national ID
  • Certificate of incorporation, shareholder register, articles
  • 6–12 months of company and personal bank statements
  • Audited accounts, usually 2 years
  • Proof of address

Requirements differ by bank and nationality. Some banks don't lend to certain nationalities. We tell you which apply to you on the first call.

How long it takes

Plan for 1 week to pre-approval and 2–3 weeks from valuation to transfer. Most of the process is remote. We plan backwards from your handover or transfer date so documents and the pre-approval don't expire on the way.
